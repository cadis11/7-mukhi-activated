Ancient Legend · The Trinity's Tear

A devotee prayed a thousand years asking which of the three gods was supreme. On the final day, all three appeared at once — and wept together, for they had never been asked to stand as one. Where their three tears fell upon the same earth, a Rudraksha tree grew, and every bead upon it bore three faces.

Ancient Legend · Six Mothers, One Son

When Shiva's radiance fell into the reeds, six sparks became six infants, each cradled by one of the Krittika mothers. Parvati gathered them in one embrace — and they became a single child with six faces: Shanmukha, who would command the armies of the gods.

Ancient Legend · Mahalakshmi's Gift

A ruined merchant wept by the Ganga. Mahalakshmi came as an old woman and placed a seven-faced bead in his palm: "This is not wealth. This is the door through which wealth flows — but first, your heart must be worthy of it." On the seventh dawn his fortunes turned. He returned to thank her; only rippling water remained, and the soft scent of lotus.
