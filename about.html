The Beginning

Born in Nepal's
Sacred Forests.

The Rudraksha tree grows in the foothills of the Himalayas, and Nepal produces the finest beads in the world — dense, tightly formed, with clear and deep mukhi lines. Every bead we offer is sourced directly from Nepal and certified for authentic mukhi formation before a single chant begins.

We do not source from markets or middlemen. Each bead is examined by hand, counted for faces, and certified. Only then does it enter the consecration process — a 54-day cycle that cannot be shortened, paused, or rushed.

The Guru

36 Lakh Chants.
A Life Given to Mantra.

🔱

Before our Guru consecrated a single bead for another person, he spent years in intensive Tantric and Vedic practice — completing 36 lakh chants of the sacred mantras associated with the Rudraksha. This is not ritual for show. It is the traditional requirement for mantra siddhi — the state in which mantra becomes living and effective.

Only after this qualification did he begin consecrating beads in batches of 12, 21, or 54 — numbers of deep significance in Vedic and Tantric tradition. Each batch receives 2 lakh chants over 54 unbroken days. No batch is released before the 54 days are complete.

"For 54 days the Guru dissolves himself into the mantra. The bead absorbs that surrender — and carries it, living, to whoever receives it."

The Guru's dakshina is built into the price of every bead. When you receive a Rudrakhchya bead, you are not buying a seed — you are receiving a transfer of consecrated energy and honouring the years of practice that made it possible.
