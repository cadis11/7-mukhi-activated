Rudrakhchya is built around two things: honest sourcing in Nepal, and a Guru whose practice stands behind every activated bead.

36
Lakh chants — Guru qualification

The Guru

Before our Guru consecrated a single bead for another person, he spent years in intensive Tantric and Vedic practice, completing 36 lakh chants of the sacred mantras associated with the Rudraksha. In this tradition that is the requirement for mantra siddhi, the state in which the mantra is considered to be alive and effective.

"For 54 days the Guru dissolves himself into the mantra. The bead absorbs that surrender — and carries it, living, to whoever receives it."

The Guru's dakshina is included in the price of every activated bead. Nothing more is asked of you.

What the 54 days contain

The consecration

१

Sourcing & Certification

Hand-selected in Nepal. Each bead is examined for authentic mukhi formation.

Origin
२

Sankalpa — The Vow

The Guru takes a formal sankalpa binding the bead to its purpose, opening the 54-day period of unbroken ritual discipline.

Vedic
३

Japa — 2,00,000 Chants

Two lakh repetitions of the bead's own beeja mantra are chanted over the bead until the count is complete.

Vedic · Tantric
४

Havan — The Fire Ritual

A sacred fire ceremony with offerings made in the bead's name, sealing the japa through Agni, the divine witness.

Vedic
५

Tantric Invocation

The ruling deity of the mukhi is invoked into the bead through Tantric sadhana and nyasa.

Tantric
६

Guru's Blessing

On the 54th day the bead is blessed and prepared for its wearer. The Guru's dakshina is included — nothing more is asked of you.

Completion

The Vedic Path

  • Sankalpa and shuddhi (purification) as the shastras describe
  • 2 lakh japa of the mukhi's Vedic beeja mantra
  • Havan — offerings through the sacred fire
  • Prana pratishtha — establishing life-force in the bead

The Tantric Path

  • Invocation of the ruling deity into the bead
  • Nyasa — placing mantra syllables upon the bead
  • 54 unbroken days of sadhana by our Guru
  • Energy sealing, so the charge travels with the bead to you
