Gaurishankar Rudraksha

Two beads joined naturally into one. In tradition, Shiva and Parvati together.

What it is

A Gaurishankar Rudraksha is two beads that have grown naturally joined at the base. Gauri is Parvati and Shankar is Shiva, and the bead is traditionally regarded as their union.

Because the joining is a natural accident of growth, a good Gaurishankar is uncommon, and the larger it is, the rarer it becomes.

Traditional associations

In tradition it is associated with harmony between partners and within the family, with steady devotion, and with the balance of Shiva and Shakti. These are statements of belief, not promises of results.

Mantra: ॐ गौरीशङ्कराय नमः (Om Gaurishankaraya Namah)

How to check a Gaurishankar

Look at the joint

  • A natural joint is uneven and fibrous
  • Two separate beads glued together show a seam or filler
  • Ask for close-up photographs of the joint

Ask for an X-ray

  • It shows whether the beads are truly joined inside
  • Recommended for large and high-value beads
  • Available from us on request

Match the paper

  • The certificate lists size and weight
  • Photographs are of the exact bead
  • Measure it when it arrives
