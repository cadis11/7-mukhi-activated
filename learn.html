Mythology · Shiva Purana

Agni &
the Three Fires

In the Vedic cosmology, Agni is not merely fire — he is the divine witness, the mediator between mortals and gods, the one who carries all offerings upward and makes transformation possible. The 3 Mukhi is Agni's direct embodiment in the material world.

The three faces represent the three fires of existence — Garhapatya (the fire of the home), Ahavaniya (the fire of the sacred offering), and Dakshina (the fire of karma). Together they represent the complete burning away of all that separates the seeker from liberation.

Once, a great sage fell into sin through pride and delusion. He performed penance for ten thousand years. Shiva wept a single tear — and where it fell, a Rudraksha tree grew with three faces on every bead. The sage wore it, and in an instant, all karma was consumed. Agni had entered the bead and made it his home.

In Tantric lineages, the 3 Mukhi is associated with Tripura Sundari, the goddess who dissolves all duality. She is called the most beautiful of the Mahavidyas — not because of appearance, but because she reveals the beauty hidden beneath all pain.

🔥 Who Should Wear the 3 Mukhi

The 3 Mukhi is for those who feel the weight of something they cannot name — a stagnation that persists despite effort, a guilt that lingers without cause, a sense that the past is still present.

Prescribed specifically for:

Mythology · Mahalakshmi's Gift

Mahalakshmi &
the Number Seven

In Vedic and Tantric tradition, seven holds immense significance — seven chakras, seven sacred rivers, seven tones of music, seven days of the week. The 7 Mukhi embodies this sacred completion.

It is a direct manifestation of Goddess Mahalakshmi. In the Shiva Purana, the Ananta Sarp (the cosmic serpent of boundlessness) presides over this bead. The 7 Mukhi is also connected to the Saptamatrikas — the seven divine mothers — invoking all seven simultaneously when worn.

A merchant, ruined and destitute, sat on the banks of the Ganga and wept. Mahalakshmi appeared as an old woman and placed a seven-faced bead in his palm. "This is not wealth," she said. "This is the door through which wealth flows. But first, your heart must be worthy of it." On the seventh dawn, his fortunes reversed. He returned to the Ganga, but she was gone — only the soft scent of lotus remained.

The bead carries the vibration of Ananga Shakti — the energy that awakens love, creation, and abundance in all aspects of life.

🪐 The Shani (Saturn) Connection

Saturn is the planet of karma, discipline, delays, and lessons. When it transits adversely — through Sade Sati, Shani Dasha, or Shani Mahadasha — its effects manifest as financial setbacks, health challenges, and a pervasive heaviness.

The 7 Mukhi is the primary remedy for Saturn in both Vedic and Tantric traditions. Prescribed for:

Sade SatiShani DashaShani Mahadasha Delayed SuccessFinancial StagnationKarmic Debt

A consecrated 7 Mukhi is believed to appease Saturn's energy and reduce suffering through these transits. Activation makes all the difference.

How to Wear

Activation &
Wearing Instructions

The following instructions apply to all Rudrakhchya beads. Our Guru activates each bead through 2 lakh mantra chants, havan, and full Tantric & Vedic ceremony — so your bead arrives already consecrated. These steps are for your personal alignment with the bead.

🌅

Monday Morning — Auspicious Timing

The ideal time to first wear your Rudraksha is Monday morning, at sunrise. Monday is governed by Shiva, and beginning on this day aligns the bead's energy with the highest Tantric and Vedic vibrations.

🛁

Cleansing Ritual

Before wearing, dip the bead in raw milk or pure water for a few minutes. Wipe gently with a clean cloth. This purifies any residual energy from transit and prepares the bead to receive your vibration.

🕉️

Chant the Beej Mantra

Hold the bead to your heart and chant: 3 Mukhi — ॐ क्लीं नमः (Om Kleem Namah) · 7 Mukhi — ॐ हुं नमः (Om Hum Namah) — 7 or 108 times. This aligns the bead to your specific energy and intention.

🪬

Wearing Position

Wear the bead touching the skin — on a red or gold thread, or a silver/gold chain, close to the heart. Do not allow others to touch your Rudraksha. Remove before sleeping if needed, and re-wear in the morning with the mantra.
