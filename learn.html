The Sacred Beads of Rudrakhchya

Mythology, deity, planet and mantra for each bead, and how to wear it. Everything here is tradition, not a promise of results.

3 Mukhi · Trimurti · Mangal

The Trinity That Frees

🔱 The Holy Trinity

The 3 Mukhi represents Brahma, Vishnu and Mahesh together — creation, preservation and dissolution in one seed.

🔥 Agni, the Unifying Fire

In tradition Agni is present in all three divine acts. The bead is said to carry the fire that burns accumulated karma.

⚡ Mars and Energy

Governed by Mars (Mangal), the planet of drive and courage. Traditionally worn by those who feel low in energy or willpower.

🌀 Release of Past Burden

Associated in tradition with letting go of guilt, heaviness and repeated stagnation.

🪐 Mangal Dosh

Jyotish tradition recommends the 3 Mukhi for those with a weak or afflicted Mars in the birth chart.

🌅 New Beginnings

Brahma creates, Vishnu sustains, Shiva dissolves — the bead is associated with inner renewal at any stage of life.

The mythology

A great devotee prayed to Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva asking which of the three was supreme. For a thousand years he received no answer. On the last day, all three appeared at once and wept together, for they had never been asked to stand as one. Where their three tears fell on the same earth, a Rudraksha tree grew, and every bead on it bore three faces.

In Tantric tradition the 3 Mukhi is also connected to Tripura Sundari, the goddess of three cities, who reveals that apparent opposites are one at the deepest level.

Beeja mantra: ॐ क्लीं नमः (Om Kleem Namah)

6 Mukhi · Kartikeya · Shukra

The Commander's Focus

⚔️ Kartikeya

The six-faced commander of the divine armies, born of Shiva's fire and raised by the six Krittika mothers.

🎯 Focus and Discipline

Traditionally worn by students and professionals who want steadier focus and willpower.

🌸 Venus (Shukra)

Linked in tradition with charm, harmony and refinement, and recommended for Shukra afflictions.

Beeja mantra: ॐ ह्रीं हुं नमः (Om Hreem Hum Namah)

7 Mukhi · Mahalakshmi · Shani

The Grace That Opens

🌺 Mahalakshmi's Grace

Governed by Mahalakshmi, the embodiment of wealth, beauty and abundance in tradition.

💰 Livelihood

Traditionally worn by people facing financial difficulty or blocked income, as a support for steadiness rather than a guarantee.

🪐 Saturn's Remedy

Recommended in Vedic and Tantric tradition during Sade Sati, Shani dasha and adverse Saturn transits.

🔱 Protection

Consecrated beads are traditionally regarded as a protective presence for the wearer.

💼 Business and Career

Worn in the hope of clarity and momentum in work, according to tradition.

🧘 Meditation

Associated with concentration and with steadier sadhana when worn consistently.

The mythology

Seven carries great weight in tradition: seven chakras, seven sacred rivers, seven tones. The 7 Mukhi is linked to the Saptamatrikas, the seven divine mothers, and, in the Shiva Purana, to Ananta Sarp, the cosmic serpent of boundlessness.

A ruined merchant wept on the banks of the Ganga. Mahalakshmi appeared as an old woman and placed a seven-faced bead in his palm. "This is not wealth. This is the door through which wealth flows — but first, your heart must be worthy of it."

Beeja mantra: ॐ हुं नमः (Om Hum Namah)

How to wear

Activation & wearing

Activated beads arrive consecrated. These steps are for your own alignment with the bead.

१

Monday sunrise

Monday is governed by Shiva, so it is the traditional day for first wearing.

२

Cleanse

Dip the bead in raw milk or clean water for a few minutes and wipe it gently with a clean cloth.

३

Chant

Hold the bead to your heart and chant its beeja mantra 7 or 108 times.

४

Wear

On a red or gold thread, or a silver or gold chain, touching the skin, close to the heart. Keep others from touching it.

३ मुखी रुद्राक्ष

त्रिमूर्तिको आशीर्वाद — कर्म मुक्तिको बाटो

३ मुखी रुद्राक्ष त्रिमूर्ति — ब्रह्मा, विष्णु र महेश — तीनै देवताहरूको प्रतीक हो। सृष्टि, पालन र संहारको एकता यो एउटै बीउमा समाहित छ भन्ने परम्परागत विश्वास छ। अग्नि यस त्रिमूर्तिको जोड्ने शक्ति हो, र मंगल ग्रहद्वारा शासित मानिन्छ।

परम्परा अनुसार यो विगतका कर्मको भार, अपराधबोध र ऊर्जाको कमीसँग जोडिएको छ। मंगल दोष वा कमजोर मंगल भएकाहरूका लागि जोतिष परम्पराले यसको सिफारिस गर्छ। मन्त्र: ॐ क्लीं नमः

७ मुखी रुद्राक्ष

महालक्ष्मीको कृपा — समृद्धिको ढोका

७ मुखी रुद्राक्ष महालक्ष्मीसँग सम्बन्धित छ र शनि ग्रहसँग जोडिएको छ। साढेसाती, शनि दशा र आर्थिक अवरोधको समयमा परम्परागत रूपमा यसको प्रयोग गरिन्छ। यसले परिणामको ग्यारेन्टी दिँदैन, श्रद्धा र साधनाको सहयोगीको रूपमा लिइन्छ। मन्त्र: ॐ हुं नमः

लगाउने विधि

पहिरने तरिका

सोमबार बिहान सूर्योदयमा रुद्राक्षलाई शुद्ध पानी वा कच्चा दूधमा केही बेर राखेर सफा कपडाले पुछ्नुहोस्। हृदयमा राखी मन्त्र ७ वा १०८ पटक जप्नुहोस्।

रातो वा सुनौलो धागो वा चाँदी/सुनको चेनमा लगाउनुहोस्, छालासँग सिधै सम्पर्कमा रहने गरी। रुद्राक्षचयाका सक्रिय (activated) दाना ५४ दिन र २ लाख मन्त्र जपसहित, हवन र तन्त्र-वैदिक विधिद्वारा अभिमन्त्रित गरिएको हुन्छ।
