Mahalakshmi and the number seven

Seven carries great weight in tradition: seven chakras, seven sacred rivers, seven tones of music, seven days of the week. The 7 Mukhi is said to be a manifestation of Mahalakshmi. In the Shiva Purana, Ananta Sarp, the cosmic serpent of boundlessness, presides over the bead. It is also linked to the Saptamatrikas, the seven divine mothers.

A merchant, ruined and destitute, sat on the banks of the Ganga and wept. Mahalakshmi appeared as an old woman and placed a seven-faced bead in his palm. "This is not wealth," she said. "This is the door through which wealth flows. But first, your heart must be worthy of it." On the seventh dawn his fortunes turned. He returned to the Ganga, but she was gone — only the soft scent of lotus remained.

The Shani (Saturn) connection

Saturn is the planet of karma, discipline and delay. When it transits adversely, through Sade Sati or a Shani dasha, tradition describes setbacks and a pervasive heaviness. The 7 Mukhi is the bead most often recommended in Vedic and Tantric tradition for these periods, and a consecrated bead is believed by devotees to help appease Saturn's energy.

How to wear

Activation & wearing

१

Monday sunrise

The traditional first wearing is on a Monday morning, the day of Shiva.

२

Cleanse

Dip in raw milk or clean water for a few minutes and wipe with a clean cloth.

३

Chant

Hold it to your heart and chant ॐ हुं नमः (Om Hum Namah) 7 or 108 times.

४

Wear

On a red or gold thread or silver or gold chain, touching the skin, close to the heart.
