The Mythology

Mahalakshmi &
the Number Seven

In the Vedic and Tantric traditions, the number seven holds immense significance — seven chakras, seven sacred rivers, seven tones of music, seven days of the week. The 7 Mukhi embodies this sacred completion.

The bead is said to be a direct manifestation of Goddess Mahalakshmi. In the Shiva Purana, the Ananta Sarp (the cosmic serpent of boundlessness) presides over this bead. The 7 Mukhi is also connected to the Saptamatrikas — the seven divine mothers — invoking all seven forms of the Goddess simultaneously when worn.

A merchant, ruined and destitute, sat on the banks of the Ganga and wept. Mahalakshmi appeared as an old woman and placed a seven-faced bead in his palm. "This is not wealth," she said. "This is the door through which wealth flows. But first, your heart must be worthy of it." On the seventh dawn his fortunes reversed entirely. He returned to the Ganga, but she was gone — only the soft scent of lotus remained.

The bead carries the vibration of Ananga Shakti — the energy that awakens love, creation, and abundance in all aspects of life.

🪐 The Shani (Saturn) Connection

Saturn is the planet of karma, discipline, delays, and lessons. When it transits adversely — through Sade Sati, Shani Dasha, or Shani Mahadasha — effects manifest as financial setbacks, health challenges, and a pervasive heaviness in life.

The 7 Mukhi is the primary remedy for Saturn in both Vedic and Tantric traditions. Prescribed for:

Sade SatiShani DashaShani Mahadasha Delayed SuccessFinancial StagnationKarmic Debt

A consecrated 7 Mukhi is believed to appease Saturn's energy and reduce the suffering associated with these transits. Activation is everything.

How to Wear

Activation &
Wearing Instructions

🌅

Monday Morning — Auspicious Timing

The ideal time to first wear your 7 Mukhi Rudraksha is Monday morning, at sunrise. Monday is governed by Shiva, and beginning on this day aligns the bead's energy with the highest Tantric and Vedic vibrations.

🛁

Cleansing Ritual

Before wearing, dip the bead in raw milk or pure water for a few minutes. Wipe gently with a clean cloth. This purifies any residual energy from transit and prepares the bead to receive your vibration.

🕉️

Chant the Beej Mantra

Hold the bead to your heart and chant: ॐ हुं नमः (Om Hum Namah) — the beej mantra of the 7 Mukhi — 7 or 108 times. This aligns the bead to your specific energy and intention.

🪬

Wearing Position

Wear the bead touching the skin — on a red or gold thread or silver/gold chain, close to the heart. Do not allow others to touch your Rudraksha. Remove before sleeping if needed, and re-wear in the morning with the mantra.
