What is Rudraksha?

Rudraksha (रुद्राक्ष) literally means "Tears of Rudra (Shiva)" in Sanskrit. According to ancient Hindu scriptures, these sacred beads formed from Lord Shiva's tears of compassion for humanity's suffering. Scientifically, Rudraksha are seeds from the Elaeocarpus ganitrus tree, found primarily in the Himalayan regions of Nepal, India, and parts of Southeast Asia.
