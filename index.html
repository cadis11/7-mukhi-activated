3 Mukhi · Agni · Mars

The Fire That
Frees the Soul

The 3 Mukhi Rudraksha is Agni himself in seed form. Three faces — three fires — representing past, present, and future. In the Shiva Purana and Padma Purana, it is described as the bead that burns accumulated karma across lifetimes, the divine flame that accepts all that should not remain and transforms it to ash.

In Tantric tradition it embodies the three Shaktis — Iccha (will), Jnana (knowledge), Kriya (action) — and the goddess Tripura Sundari, the most beautiful of the Dasha Mahavidyas. It is the bead of transformation before abundance.

Governing planet: Mangal (Mars). For those feeling stuck despite effort, carrying guilt or heaviness from the past, facing Mars afflictions in their chart, or standing at a major crossroads in life — this bead burns what must go.

Ancient Legend · Shiva Purana

Once, a great sage fell into sin through pride and delusion. He performed penance for ten thousand years, his body still and unmoving like a pillar of stone. Shiva, moved by his devotion, wept a single tear — and where it fell upon the earth, a Rudraksha tree grew with three faces on every bead. The sage wore it, and in an instant, all karma — ten thousand lifetimes of it — was consumed. Agni had entered the bead and made it his home. Thus the 3 Mukhi became known as the liberator of those burdened by what they cannot name.

7 Mukhi · Mahalakshmi · Saturn

The Grace That
Opens the Path

The 7 Mukhi Rudraksha is ruled by Maa Mahalakshmi — goddess of abundance, grace, and divine prosperity — and governed by Shani (Saturn). Seven faces representing the seven seas, the seven divine rivers, the seven rays of the sun. In the Shiva Purana, the Ananta Sarp (the cosmic serpent) presides over this bead, its boundlessness a mirror for the wealth it can bring.

In Tantric tradition, the 7 Mukhi is deeply connected to the Saptamatrikas — the seven divine mothers. Wearing it is said to invoke all seven forms of the Goddess simultaneously, aligning the wearer's energy with the complete spectrum of abundance: material, spiritual, relational, and karmic.

Especially prescribed for Sade Sati, Shani dasha, and those experiencing prolonged financial stagnation. Where the 3 Mukhi clears the path, the 7 Mukhi opens the door.

Ancient Legend · Mahalakshmi's Gift

It is told in the ancient texts that a merchant, ruined and destitute, sat on the banks of the Ganga and wept. Mahalakshmi appeared to him as an old woman carrying a garland of Rudraksha beads. She placed a single bead — seven-faced — in his palm and said: "This is not wealth. This is the door through which wealth flows. But first, your heart must be worthy of it." The merchant performed penance for seven days wearing the bead, and on the seventh dawn his fortunes reversed entirely. He returned to the Ganga, but the old woman was gone. Only the rippling water remained — and the soft scent of lotus.
