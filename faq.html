Frequently asked

Short answers to the things people ask us most. If yours is missing, message us.

Ordering & delivery

How do I order?

Choose your bead on the Shop page and press Order. It opens WhatsApp with your choice already written. We confirm availability, and you confirm payment and delivery details with us before anything is sent.

Do you ship outside Nepal?

Yes. Delivery within Nepal is free. For India and the rest of the world, message us your country and we confirm the shipping cost before you commit to anything.

Which bead is right for me?

Message us your questions or birth details and we will talk it through. The Sacred Beads page also explains what each mukhi is traditionally associated with.

Natural vs activated

What is the difference between natural and activated?

A natural certified bead is the genuine Nepal-sourced bead with its certificate, not ceremonially consecrated. An activated bead goes through our Guru's 54-day Vedic and Tantric consecration with 2 lakh chants and havan, and costs more because of that work and the Guru's dakshina.

Can I activate a natural bead later?

Yes. If you start with a natural bead and later want the consecration, message us and we will arrange it.

What is the pendant option?

For NRS 3,000 extra, your bead comes set in a Panchadhatu Om locket pendant that opens to hold it, so it is ready to wear.

Authenticity

How do I know the bead is genuine?

Every bead comes with a certificate recording its number, size and mukhi count, matched to photographs of that exact bead. See the Authenticity page for how to inspect a bead yourself.

Do you provide X-ray reports?

X-ray reports are available for rare or high-value beads and on request. Ask us on WhatsApp.

Wearing & care

How do I wear it?

Traditionally the first wearing is on a Monday morning at sunrise, after a gentle cleansing, with the beeja mantra chanted 7 or 108 times. See the Sacred Beads page for the full steps.

How do I care for the bead?

Keep it away from harsh chemicals, perfume and soaking in water for long periods. Wipe it with a soft dry cloth, and a little natural oil now and then keeps the surface supple.
