Ordering & delivery

How do I order?

Choose your bead on the Shop page and press Order. It opens WhatsApp with your choice already written. We confirm availability, and you confirm payment and delivery details with us before anything is sent.

Do you ship outside Nepal?

Yes. Delivery within Nepal is free. For India and the rest of the world, message us your country and we confirm the shipping cost before you commit to anything.

Which bead is right for me?

Message us your questions or birth details and we will talk it through. The Sacred Beads page also explains what each mukhi is traditionally associated with.

Energized vs activated

What is the difference between energized and activated?

An energized bead is a genuine Nepal-sourced Rudraksha that has been energized and is ready to wear, from NRS 1,500. An activated bead goes through our Guru's full 54-day Vedic and Tantric consecration with 2 lakh chants and havan. It costs more because of the Guru's time, years of practice and dakshina.

What is the locket option?

Your bead comes set in a Panchadhatu Om locket pendant that opens to hold it, so it is ready to wear. An energized bead in the locket is NRS 2,500.

What is Gaurishankar Rudraksha?

Two beads joined naturally, traditionally seen as the union of Shiva (Shankar) and Parvati (Gauri). It is rare, so it is priced by size, from NRS 18,000.

Authenticity

How do I know the bead is genuine?

Activated and Gaurishankar beads come with a certificate matched to photographs of that exact bead. For any bead you can ask us for close-up photos or a video first. See the Authenticity page for how to inspect a bead yourself.

Do you provide X-ray reports?

X-ray reports are available for rare or high-value beads, such as large Gaurishankar, and on request. Ask us on WhatsApp.

Wearing & care

How do I wear it?

Traditionally the first wearing is on a Monday morning at sunrise, after a gentle cleansing, with the beeja mantra chanted 7 or 108 times. See the Sacred Beads page for the full steps.

How do I care for the bead?

Keep it away from harsh chemicals, perfume and soaking in water for long periods. Wipe it with a soft dry cloth, and a little natural oil now and then keeps the surface supple.
